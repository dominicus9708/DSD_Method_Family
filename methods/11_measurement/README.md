# 11. DSD Measurement / DSD 측정론

Status: **proposed**

Task: determine which observations or measurements are needed to distinguish structurally relevant alternatives at a declared resolution.

Primary DSD sources: describability conditions, typed applicability, first branching, static readouts, and dynamic distinguishability support.

Typical outputs:
- measurable target and resolution declaration;
- candidate measurements mapped to discriminating structure;
- measurements that cannot distinguish the alternatives;
- missing-data and inapplicability status rules;
- measurement-to-claim bridge assumptions.

Boundary: DSD Measurement does not replace metrology, experimental design, clinical measurement standards, or field-specific instrumentation theory.
