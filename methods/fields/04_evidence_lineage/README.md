# Field IV. Evidence & Lineage / 분야 IV. 증거·계보

Classification role only. The methods below remain independent.

분류용 상위 분야이며, 아래 방법들은 서로 독립적으로 유지합니다.

- [`Measurement / DSD 측정론`](../../11_measurement/) — design observations needed to distinguish relevant alternatives.
- [`Provenance / DSD 출처·유래 추적론`](../../09_provenance_lineage/provenance/) — record origin, derivation, transmission, source, and version chain.
- [`Lineage / DSD 계보론`](../../09_provenance_lineage/lineage/) — record predecessor/successor identity across change.

Evidence availability, provenance, and lineage are different claims. A measured datum may have uncertain provenance; known provenance does not by itself prove lineage.
