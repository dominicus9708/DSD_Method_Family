# Field V. Reduction & Representation / 분야 V. 축약·표현

Classification role only. The methods below remain independent.

분류용 상위 분야이며, 아래 방법들은 서로 독립적으로 유지합니다.

- [`Aggregation / DSD 집계론`](../../10_aggregation_compression/aggregation/) — construct a declared representative value or readout from admitted data.
- [`Compression / DSD 압축론`](../../10_aggregation_compression/compression/) — reduce representation size, resolution, or retained detail subject to purpose-required distinguishability.

Aggregation may increase convenience without reducing representation in the same sense as compression. Compression quality depends on what distinctions must remain recoverable or task-relevant.
