# PHY-QM-018 — Unread outcome carrier vs physical dephasing

Date: 2026-09-06

## Question

Can a quantum measurement outcome carrier that exists but is not read be treated as equivalent to a carrier that has physically decohered?

## External quantum specialization

Use the coherent premeasurement state

\[
|\Phi^+\rangle_{SR}=\frac{|00\rangle+|11\rangle}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
\rho_C=|\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|.
\]

Here `S` is the measured qubit and `R` is the outcome carrier. The fully dephased control in the carrier pointer basis is

\[
\rho_D=\frac12|00\rangle\langle00|+\frac12|11\rangle\langle11|.
\]

The dephasing channel is an external standard-quantum operation; DSD does not derive it.

## Marginal collision

Both states have identical subsystem marginals:

\[
\rho_S=\rho_R=I/2.
\]

Numerically,

```text
trace distance full(C,D) = 0.5
trace distance S marginals = 0
trace distance R marginals = 0
```

Thus local reduced readouts cannot distinguish the two global states.

The mutual information differs:

\[
I_C(S:R)=2\ \text{bits},
\qquad
I_D(S:R)=1\ \text{bit}.
\]

The coherent state retains quantum correlation that the dephased state does not.

## Reversal witness

The premeasurement was generated by a CNOT from

\[
|+\rangle_S|0\rangle_R.
\]

Applying the inverse CNOT gives recovery fidelity

\[
F_{\rm rec}(\rho_C)=1,
\qquad
F_{\rm rec}(\rho_D)=\frac12.
\]

Therefore the same local marginals do not determine whether the premeasurement can be coherently reversed.

## Conditional X-basis carrier readout

Measure `R` in the X basis. For the coherent state,

\[
R_X=+ \Rightarrow \rho_S=|+\rangle\langle+|,
\qquad
R_X=- \Rightarrow \rho_S=|-\rangle\langle-|,
\]

with probability `1/2` each. The conditional off-diagonal magnitude is

\[
|\rho_{S,01}|=1/2.
\]

For the dephased state, either X-basis carrier outcome leaves

\[
\rho_S=I/2,
\qquad
|\rho_{S,01}|=0.
\]

This is a finite quantum-eraser-style recoverability witness: an unread coherent carrier and a physically dephased carrier can agree on local readouts while differing under a later admissible protocol.

## DSD factorization obstruction

Let

\[
\Phi_{\rm local}(\rho)=(\rho_S,\rho_R)
\]

and let `R_eraser` denote the later conditional-coherence/recovery response. Then

\[
\Phi_{\rm local}(\rho_C)=\Phi_{\rm local}(\rho_D)
\]

but

\[
R_{\rm eraser}(\rho_C)\ne R_{\rm eraser}(\rho_D).
\]

Hence no function `g` can satisfy

\[
R_{\rm eraser}=g\circ\Phi_{\rm local}
\]

on this witness family.

## Audit verdict

- `not read = dephased`: **FAIL**.
- `same local marginals => same future protocol response`: **FAIL**.
- local reduced state is a complete descriptor of measurement lineage: **FAIL**.
- coherent carrier existence and observer access should be typed separately: **PASS**.
- physical dephasing belongs to a downstream dynamical/quantum bridge, not a Property-core status: **PASS**.

Verdict: **PASS_WITH_BOUNDARY** for DSD. The existing DSD separation between reduced readout and component-resolved dynamic state survives the quantum control.
