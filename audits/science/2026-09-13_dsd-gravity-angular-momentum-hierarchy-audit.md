## DSD-side constraint

The current DSD papers do not permit a property name or scalar aggregate to be promoted to a physical vector/tensor charge by label alone. Typed inputs, status distinctions, explicit association/localization, and an application-specific constitutive bridge must be supplied. Conservation is also not a generic DSD theorem.

Therefore neither a scalar property value nor a momentum-like channel label is automatically the `J` that appears in Kerr or Kerr-Newman.

## Angular-momentum hierarchy

The active hierarchy is

\[
J0\to J1\to J2\to J3\to J4\to J5.
\]

### J0 — DSD typed property / channel data

A DSD record may contain a declared value and complete typed inputs. At this layer there is no automatic spatial position, origin, rotation axis, physical momentum unit, or angular-momentum interpretation.

### J1 — local physical momentum / stress / flux data

After an explicit physical-relativistic bridge, local records may receive meanings such as momentum density, energy flux, stress, spin current, or matter current. These meanings are R2/R4 specialization data rather than generic DSD primitives.

### J2 — slice-relative matter angular momentum

Once a spatial slice, localization map, origin/axis convention, and physical momentum representation are supplied, an orbital contribution has the ordinary flat-space witness form

\[
\mathbf J_{\rm orb}=\sum_i (\mathbf r_i-\mathbf O)\times\mathbf p_i,
\]

or the appropriate covariant/symmetry-current generalization in relativistic field theory.

This level already depends on more than scalar energy and total momentum.

### J3 — conserved symmetry angular momentum

A conserved angular-momentum charge requires an appropriate rotational symmetry/current and the conservation assumptions needed by the chosen external matter/relativity model. It is not generated by DSD typing alone.

### J4 — global spacetime angular momentum

For an isolated asymptotically flat gravitating system, total angular momentum is a global geometric/boundary charge defined only after sufficient asymptotic structure and frame conditions are supplied. In stationary axisymmetric settings a suitably normalized Komar integral associated with the axial Killing field provides the corresponding angular-momentum component.

This layer must remain distinct from local matter angular momentum because a vacuum exterior can have `T_{mu nu}=0` while the spacetime still carries nonzero black-hole angular momentum.

### J5 — black-hole solution parameter

Only after a valid global relativistic angular momentum has been obtained may it enter an external Kerr/Kerr-Newman comparator through

\[
\chi=\frac{Jc}{GM^2}.
\]

Kerr is therefore a downstream comparator, not the target imposed on DSD.

## Localization witness

Consider two externally specialized one-particle states with equal scalar energy and equal total linear momentum:

State A:

\[
\mathbf r_A=(0,1,0),\qquad \mathbf p_A=(1,0,0),
\]

State B:

\[
\mathbf r_B=(0,-1,0),\qquad \mathbf p_B=(1,0,0).
\]

Both have the same energy proxy and

\[
\mathbf P_A=\mathbf P_B=(1,0,0),
\]

but

\[
J_{z,A}=-1,\qquad J_{z,B}=+1.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{(E,\mathbf P)\ \text{do not determine}\ \mathbf J\ \text{without localization/origin data}.}
\]

This is a direct obstruction to assigning Kerr `J` from a reduced DSD scalar or momentum aggregate.

## Origin dependence witness

Under a constant shift of origin by vector `a`,

\[
\mathbf r_i' = \mathbf r_i-\mathbf a,
\]

so

\[
\mathbf J' = \mathbf J-\mathbf a\times\mathbf P_{\rm tot}.
\]

Therefore orbital angular momentum is origin-dependent whenever total linear momentum is nonzero.

A control with

\[
\mathbf P_{\rm tot}=0
\]

removes this constant-origin shift term. This shows that a legitimate global `J` bridge must also fix the asymptotic/rest-frame and center/origin convention appropriate to the external relativistic construction instead of hiding it inside a scalar label.

## Matter J is not automatically total spacetime J

The black-hole exterior gives the decisive separation. In a vacuum exterior,

\[
T_{\mu\nu}=0,
\]

but Kerr still carries nonzero total angular momentum. Hence

\[
\boxed{J_{\rm matter,local}\neq J_{\rm spacetime,global}\quad\text{in general}.}
\]

A separate asymptotic/boundary or symmetry construction is required.
