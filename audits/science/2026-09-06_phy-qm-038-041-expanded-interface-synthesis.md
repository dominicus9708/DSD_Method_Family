## PHY-QM-038 — Four independent diagnostic axes

The accumulated audits show that a single scalar 'describability' score is insufficient for quantum/relativistic applications. At minimum, the following axes must remain separate.

### A. Readout identifiability

Does the declared readout map distinguish the underlying admissible states?

`ker Phi = 0` or the corresponding nonlinear fiber criterion.

### B. Model-class completability

Does the observed/read-out structure belong to a supplied classical or restricted model class?

Examples: noncontextual, separable, LHS, Bell-local.

### C. Recoverability / sufficiency

Can omitted structure be reconstructed from the retained coordinates by an allowed recovery map?

Examples: Petz/Markov recovery, quantum error correction.

### D. Accessibility / causal availability

Is the relevant carrier/readout available under the actual observer protocol, measurement class, spatial support, and causal history?

Examples: steering direction, hidden steering, environment fragments, QFT local algebras, DSD c_info specialization.

None of A-D implies the others in general.

## PHY-QM-039 — Specialization hierarchy after the expansion

The safest hierarchy is now:

1. DSD Formation: admitted structural material/channels.
2. DSD Property: ordered typed inputs, applicability, prerequisites, partial assignments.
3. DSD static/readout layer: reduced aggregates and reconstruction tests.
4. Domain representation: Hilbert space, density operators, local algebras, tensor factors when justified.
5. Domain dynamics: unitary evolution, quantum instruments, CPTP maps, QFT local dynamics.
6. Domain causal structure: Bell/no-signalling distinctions, relativistic spacelike locality, microcausality.
7. Observer/protocol layer: allowed measurements, roles, filtering, adaptive history, accessible environment fragments.
8. Recovery/completion diagnostics: contextuality, separability, LHS, Bell locality, Markov recovery, QEC.

This hierarchy is more robust than directly interpreting a DSD property label as a physical law.

## PHY-QM-040 — Candidate general DSD-audit rules supported by multiple domains

The following rules now have repeated support and are candidates for promotion into the general DSD audit methodology, not into the foundational axioms.

1. **Compression audit:** a reduced descriptor may not replace the full typed/support record without an injectivity or factorization theorem.
2. **Completion audit:** distinguish failure to reconstruct the underlying state from failure to fit a supplied model class.
3. **Recovery audit:** local loss/corruption and irreversible global loss are different unless a recovery impossibility theorem is supplied.
4. **Protocol audit:** a property can depend on operation order, adaptive policy, conditioning, and role direction.
5. **Support audit:** information amount and information distribution over supports are different coordinates.
6. **Accessibility audit:** physical existence and observer-accessible/readable status are different coordinates.
7. **Causality audit:** correlation/nonclassicality/no-signalling and finite propagation must be tested separately.
8. **Representation audit:** the availability of a tensor-product or geometric representation must be proved/supplied for the regime; do not promote it universally from one successful specialization.

## PHY-QM-041 — Current expanded contact map

The DSD contact with standard physics now has two distinct shapes.

### Quantum mechanics / quantum information

DSD is useful primarily as a decomposition and audit framework for:

- context-indexed identifiability;
- global completion obstructions;
- subsystem factorization and entanglement;
- role-sensitive steering;
- Bell-local completion versus no-signalling;
- sequential instrument lineage;
- decoherence and record accessibility;
- environment redundancy;
- conditional sufficiency/recovery;
- error-corrected logical information.

DSD does not derive the Hilbert-space postulates, Born rule, CP maps, no-cloning theorem, QEC conditions, or quantum Markov theorems. Those remain external physics/mathematics bridges.

### Relativity / relativistic QFT

DSD is useful primarily as a decomposition and audit framework for:

- representation change versus structural information loss;
- causal accessibility versus mathematical describability;
- finite propagation versus no-signalling;
- local algebras and spacelike commutativity;
- sharp localization versus subsystem factorization;
- conditional subsystem recovery through split/type-I bridges.

DSD does not derive Lorentz invariance, the relativistic light cone, microcausality, the local algebra net, the spectrum condition, or the split property.

## Overall verdict

The expansion strengthens the interpretation of DSD as a **typed structural audit and describability framework** rather than as a replacement for standard quantum mechanics or relativity.

The strongest surviving common pattern is:

`complete typed/support structure`
`-> declared representation/readout`
`-> explicit loss/fiber analysis`
`-> supplied domain model class`
`-> recovery/completion/accessibility tests`
`-> causal/protocol audit`.

This is already mathematically nontrivial and externally testable without claiming that DSD has derived the standard physical laws themselves.
