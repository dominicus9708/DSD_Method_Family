## 2. Non-retroactive compatibility rules for newly initiated method applications
**F1 / Formation T4.7:** invoke independence only for the six *residual primitive constraints* after typing-induced dependencies (P4.6) are removed. Do not elevate that result to independence of every unreduced clause or of every parent axiom considered as an indivisible statement. Activation: proof-audit/axiom-independence claims and derivation tracking.

**F2 / Formation P5.13:** zero-extension alone does not necessarily make admitted-channel membership unrecoverable from outputs. The proved collision requires both an absent candidate and an admitted zero-term channel. Distinguish `CHANNEL_ABSENCE`, `ADMITTED_ZERO_TERM`, and nonzero components. Activation: Analysis, Specification, Aggregation, Compression, Reconstruction, Measurement, Synthesis and any method consuming their outputs. For an arbitrary new case require the existential collision premise before asserting information loss from the proposed zero extension.

**F3 / Formation §7.3 D2:** the 3×3 carrier has 9 vertex + 12 edge + 36 directional-slot = 57 material items; the selected h* has 2 vertices + 1 edge + 6 exposed blocked slots = 9 items. Blocked annotations are *supplied material inputs*, inherited through restrictions, not generated by grid geometry. The source's b(L+,L−)=3 is a specified finite witness; the method family cannot promote that witness to a generic empirical boundary law or infer first branching from first observation alone. Activation: first-branch Comparison, Measurement, Computation, Design, Classification and case-specific modelling.

**F4 / Formation §8:** strict base-fixed formation isomorphism gives finite-family composition transport under its *associated* term-space isomorphism. It does not force equal composites under an arbitrarily and separately prescribed linear isomorphism J. Conversely equal composite outputs under a fixed J do not imply strict formation equivalence. Activation: Comparison, Aggregation, Compression, Synthesis, Reconstruction, transformation/reduction and any equivalence-based Optimization.
