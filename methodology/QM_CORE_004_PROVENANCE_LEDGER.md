## 1. Purpose

This ledger prevents two claims from being conflated:

```text
A/B structure reached independently by DSD/general operational reasoning

versus

C/D structure supplied or introduced while matching quantum theory or an exact comparator theorem
```

A successful conditional reconstruction using C/D inputs is compatibility evidence, not independent derivation evidence.
