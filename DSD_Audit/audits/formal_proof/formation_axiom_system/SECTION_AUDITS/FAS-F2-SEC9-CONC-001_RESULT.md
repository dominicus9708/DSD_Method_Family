## 2. The §8 Discussion problem does not contaminate the exact §9 claim

Previous audit **FAS-F2-SEC8-DISC-001** identified one §8 phrase stating that fixed-J composite coincidence is “therefore strictly weaker than strict formation equivalence.” Its unlimited logical-entailment interpretation is **not true** under Definition6.21: a prescribed F-linear J and nonempty-family requirement mean strict equivalence need not imply that independently fixed-J coincidence (one-channel pair with J=−id, or strict zero-channel pair with J=id).

**Original §9 wording is different**. The final Conclusion clause says an indexed equality of composite outputs **can coexist with strict formation non-equivalence**. It says only that a concrete instance exists, which §7.4 demonstrably provides, and does **not** state the erroneous converse logical entailment. Therefore:

- §9 last-sentence mathematics: **SUPPORTED**; **do not change or weaken it merely to propagate §8’s correction**.
- §8 D10: its own **required logical-scope clarification remains OPEN IN THE UNCHANGED MANUSCRIPT** and must be addressed separately before any revised release.
- Proposition6.22: unaltered valid proof of coincidence-not-implying-strict-isomorphism; no dependency retraction.

A conclusion-level claim of “more structure information” would be explanatory rather than a formal implication hierarchy; §9 does not need one.
